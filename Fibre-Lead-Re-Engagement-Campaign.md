# Campaign: Fibre Lead Re-Engagement

## Campaign Purpose

This campaign is designed to re-engage 1,719 existing leads who previously received a Telkom Fibre sales message via WhatsApp but did not respond.

The purpose of the campaign is to determine whether these customers now have an interest in Telkom Fibre and, where interest is confirmed, move the customer into the sales calling process.

**Day 2 – Monitoring Period**

- No additional marketing message should be sent.
- Layla's role is to monitor and process any responses received from customers who received Campaign 1.
- Customers who respond must be categorised according to the campaign rules below.
- Customers who do not respond remain in a **PENDING** status.

**Day 3 – Final Re-Engagement Message**

Only customers who:

1. Received Campaign 1;
2. Did not respond;
3. Did not indicate that they are not interested; and
4. Did not opt out by replying STOP

should receive the final re-engagement message.

**Approved Message**

> Hi, Just following up on the Fibre offer we previously sent you 😊
>
> Before we close off our follow-up, is there anything you would like us to clarify or assist you with before making a decision?
>
> You can reply with:
>
> 1 – I'd like more information
> 2 – I'd like to speak to a consultant
> 3 – I'm interested, please contact me
> 4 – I'm not interested
> STOP – Do not contact me again

**Day 4–5 – Response Processing**

No additional promotional messages should be sent unless required as part of an approved response-handling flow.

Layla should process responses based on the customer's intent:

**INTERESTED / READY TO PROCEED**
- Confirm interest
- Collect required customer information
- Mark INTERESTED
- Move to CALLING QUEUE

**WANTS TO SPEAK TO A CONSULTANT**
- Ask for a preferred callback time
- Collect any outstanding required information
- Mark INTERESTED / CALLBACK REQUESTED
- Move to CALLING QUEUE

**REQUIRES MORE INFORMATION / HAS QUESTIONS**
- Identify what information or assistance the customer requires
- Provide the relevant information where Layla is able to do so
- Continue the conversation to determine whether genuine interest develops
- If genuine interest is established, mark INTERESTED and move to CALLING QUEUE
- If the customer remains undecided, record the outcome appropriately and remove them from the active campaign

**NOT INTERESTED**
- Where appropriate, ask what is preventing the customer from proceeding
- Record the reason/objection
- Mark NOT INTERESTED
- Remove from active campaign

**NO RESPONSE**
- Once the final campaign message has been sent and no response is received
- Mark NO RESPONSE – FINAL ATTEMPT
- Remove from the active campaign
- Add the customer to the FUTURE RE-MARKETING / NURTURE POOL
- Retain the campaign history against the customer

**STOP / OPT-OUT**
- Mark OPTED OUT
- Immediately stop all marketing communication
- Exclude the customer from future campaign messaging and nurture campaigns

**Day 6 – Campaign Closure**

Customers who have not responded to either Campaign 1 or the Final Re-Engagement Message must be marked:

**NO RESPONSE – FINAL ATTEMPT**

These customers should be removed from the active campaign and moved to the:

**FUTURE RE-MARKETING POOL**

Layla must not continue sending messages to these customers as part of this campaign.

## 2. Campaign Rules

### Who is receiving the campaign?

The campaign is specifically for 1,719 existing leads who:

- Previously received a Telkom Fibre sales message;
- Did not respond to the previous message; and
- Are being re-engaged with a new Fibre offer.

This is a defined lead segment and should not automatically include new leads or customers from other campaigns.

### Campaign Schedule

The campaign consists of two customer-facing marketing messages:

| Stage | Timing | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Campaign 1 | Day 1 | Send new Fibre offer |
| Monitoring | Day 2 | Monitor and process responses; no new marketing message |
| Final Re-Engagement | Day 3 | Send final message only to qualifying non-responders |
| Processing | Day 4–5 | Process final responses |
| Closure | Day 6 | Close campaign and assign final outcome |

The exact campaign start date will be provided before deployment.

The campaign should remain active until all leads have received their appropriate final status.

---

## 3. Number of Follow-Ups

This campaign should **not** use the standard five-step follow-up sequence.

It is intentionally limited to two customer-facing campaign opportunities:

1. Initial Re-Engagement Message
2. Final Re-Engagement Message

The purpose is to re-engage the customer without repeatedly messaging them.

**The key rule is:**

> Each customer receives a maximum of two campaign messages unless the customer actively engages in the conversation and a response is required to complete the sales process.

Once a customer reaches a final outcome, Layla must stop campaign messaging.

---

## 4. Customer Response Rules

Layla must categorise every response received.

### Interested

Examples may include:

- "Yes"
- "I'm interested"
- "I want fibre"
- "How do I apply?"
- "Please call me"
- "I would like the R425 package"
- "Can someone contact me?"

**Action:** INTERESTED → Collect required information → CALLING QUEUE

### Customer Asks a Question

Layla should answer using only approved product, pricing and process information.

Examples:

- Asking about package pricing
- Asking about installation
- Asking about contract duration
- Asking about speed
- Asking whether Fibre is available
- Asking how to apply

Layla should continue the conversation appropriately.

- If the customer subsequently demonstrates genuine interest: INTERESTED → CALLING QUEUE
- If the customer declines: NOT INTERESTED → Record reason → Close

### Not Interested

If the customer clearly declines:

**NOT INTERESTED → Record reason → Remove from active campaign**

Where appropriate, Layla should ask the reason for declining so that the reason can be captured for campaign analysis.

### STOP

If the customer replies "STOP" or clearly requests that marketing communication cease:

**OPTED OUT → Immediately stop marketing communication**

The customer must not receive the remaining campaign message.

### No Response

- If there is no response after the first message: PENDING → Final Re-Engagement Message
- If there is still no response after the final message: NO RESPONSE – FINAL ATTEMPT → FUTURE RE-MARKETING POOL

---

## 5. What Does Success Look Like?

The primary objective is not simply receiving a response.

A response is considered an engagement signal, but the campaign's ultimate success is measured by the customer's progression through the sales funnel.

### Primary Success Outcome

A lead is considered a successful campaign conversion when the customer:

- Demonstrates genuine interest in the Fibre offering;
- Provides the required information through the authorised process;
- Agrees to be contacted by a sales consultant; and/or
- Proceeds with a Fibre application.

**The ultimate commercial objective is:**

> LEAD → ENGAGED → INTERESTED → SALES CALL → APPLICATION → SALE

---

## 6. Calling Queue Qualification

A customer should be moved to the CALLING QUEUE when there is sufficient evidence of genuine purchase intent.

Examples include:

- Customer explicitly requests a call;
- Customer asks to apply;
- Customer selects a Fibre package and wants to proceed;
- Customer provides the required contact information through the authorised process;
- Customer asks a sales consultant to assist with the application.

Layla should **not** place a customer into the calling queue simply because they responded to the message.

The response must indicate genuine interest or a request for sales assistance.

---

## 7. Information Layla Should Capture

For an interested customer, Layla should capture, where required and through the authorised process:

- Full Name & Surname
- Contact Number
- Email Address
- Preferred Fibre Package
- Customer's stated interest/request
- Preferred callback time, where applicable
- Campaign source
- Campaign stage
- Date/time of interaction
- Final customer outcome

Sensitive information such as ID numbers, banking details, passwords or OTPs must not be requested through ordinary WhatsApp chat and must instead be collected through the approved secure process.

---

## 8. Required Final Status for Every Lead

Every lead must eventually have one final recorded outcome.

The available outcomes should include:

- INTERESTED – CALLING QUEUE
- CALLBACK REQUESTED – CALLING QUEUE
- NOT INTERESTED
- OPTED OUT
- NO RESPONSE – FINAL ATTEMPT
- OTHER / INVALID LEAD, where applicable

There must be no leads left indefinitely in an undefined status.

---

## 9. Key Rule for Layla

**Do not repeatedly message the same customer.**

The campaign is designed around controlled re-engagement.

Each lead receives a maximum of two campaign opportunities. Layla must monitor the customer's behaviour, record the outcome and stop campaign communication once the customer reaches a final status.

The purpose of Layla is not merely to send messages. She must identify customer intent, categorise the lead, capture the relevant information, and move qualified interested customers into the human sales process.

### The most important rule to add

Layla needs to distinguish between **"engagement"** and **"sales-qualified interest."**

For example:

> Customer: "How much is the 50 Mbps package?"

That is not yet a sale or necessarily a calling-queue lead. Layla should answer the question and continue the conversation.

But:

> Customer: "I want the 50 Mbps package. Please have someone call me."

That is clearly **INTERESTED → CALLING QUEUE**.

This distinction will make reporting much more valuable because we will be able to see the complete funnel:

> 1,719 leads → Responses → Engaged → Interested → Calling Queue → Applications → Sales
